About us

Established in 2017 with a few individuals who wanted to improve the awareness of the quality engineering community in Sri Lanka.

We started by introducing hands-on training sessions for the quality community through Code Camps.

Throughout the years we have organized many events which helped to improve the technical knowledge of the QA community in Sri Lanka.

Got Questions?

Find answers to commonly asked questions about CQC and our activities.

CQC stands for Colombo Quality Camps. We are a group of enthusiasts dedicated to improving quality awareness in the Sri Lankan quality assurance community. We organize events, meetups, and sessions, and also provide consultations for all your quality assurance needs.
We wanted to make a meaningful contribution to the QA community. We identified a gap for workshops in the Quality Engineering space and started our journey in 2017 with our first quality camp — which was a huge success. Since then, we have organized various sessions, meetups, and our signature annual event, Quality Week.
Quality Week is our signature annual event. We organize it each year to advance the knowledge of the quality assurance community in Sri Lanka. We invite distinguished speakers from Sri Lanka and internationally to present at this prestigious event.
Joining the CQC community is easy! You can attend our public events and meetups, follow us on social media, and reach out through our contact page to get more involved. Our events are open to everyone passionate about quality engineering.
We run hands-on bootcamps and technical sessions covering topics like test automation, software quality methodologies, performance testing, and more. Our sessions are designed for QA professionals at all levels — from beginners to experienced practitioners.
Yes! We offer quality engineering consultations for teams and companies of all sizes. Our experienced practitioners can help you improve your testing processes, implement quality best practices, and level up your QA team's capabilities.
